Research is great, but there's more to life than CSI signals and confusion matrices! 😄 When I step away from my laptop, I'm usually doing one of two things: **reading a book 📚** or **moving my body 💪**.


## 📚 The Bookworm Side

My reading list is a strange but wonderful mix of **history 🏛️**, **quantum physics ⚛️** and **religion 🕌**. What fascinates me most is where these worlds meet: how ancient ideas about consciousness, faith and reality line up with what modern physics is discovering. Give me a book that connects the dots between them and I'll happily lose track of time.

### My all-time favourites

| Book | Author | Why I love it |
|------|--------|---------------|
| ✨ *The Secret* | Rhonda Byrne | The book that got me thinking about how our thoughts shape our reality. |
| 🙏 *The Magic* | Rhonda Byrne | A 28-day journey into gratitude. A small daily habit with a surprisingly big effect. |
| 🧠 *Becoming Supernatural* | Dr. Joe Dispenza | Where meditation meets neuroscience and quantum physics. Right in my sweet spot! |
| 🔄 *Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself* | Dr. Joe Dispenza | A practical guide to letting go of old patterns and rewiring the mind. |
| 🪞 *The Mirror Book* | | A reminder to look inward and reflect on who you really are. |

> 💬 Have a book recommendation on history, physics or the big questions of life? I'm always looking for my next read, so send it my way!


## 💪 The Active Side

A sharp mind needs a strong body (and a calm one too 🧘). Here's where you can find me:

- 🧘 **Meditation group:** my reset button. Quieting the mind is harder than training any neural network!
- 🏋️ **The gym:** lifting, sweating and clearing my head after long debugging sessions.
- 🏏 **The cricket field:** of course! Bat, ball and good friends make the perfect afternoon.
- 🦾 **Pull-ups:** my all-time favourite exercise. Simple, honest and always a challenge. Just you versus gravity!


*Fun fact: a good workout or a good book is often the best debugging tool there is.* 🚀
