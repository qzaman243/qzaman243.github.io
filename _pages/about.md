Hello! I am Qamar Zaman, a Computer Engineer. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan.

I am currently working with [Dr. Sagheer Khan](https://pure.kfupm.edu.sa/en/persons/sagheer-khan/) (SDAIA–KFUPM Joint Research Center for Artificial Intelligence, King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals) on the Internet of Things (IoT), the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) and digital twins for healthcare.

My research focuses on contactless human sensing with WiFi. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with [Dr. Muhammad Salman](https://salmanpolito.github.io/) and Dr. Sagheer Khan on occupancy detection using WiFi Channel State Information (CSI). I built a real-time sensing system on a Raspberry Pi with Nexmon. This work led to a first-author journal paper, [PiSense](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00607-026-01751-9), in _Computing_ (Springer, 2026), and a public [CSI dataset on IEEE DataPort](https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/csi-dataset-wifi-based-human-detection).

I also have hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning and NLP. My hardware background includes embedded Linux and edge AI deployment.

**Research interests**

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Wireless Sensing
- Edge Computing and Edge Intelligence
