A **digital twin** is a living virtual copy of a real patient, organ or care environment. It is kept up to date with data from sensors and wearables, and it uses AI and simulation to predict what will happen next. In healthcare, this could help doctors monitor patients continuously, test treatments virtually before applying them, and catch problems earlier.

In this work, together with [Dr. Sagheer Khan](https://pure.kfupm.edu.sa/en/persons/sagheer-khan/) (SDAIA–KFUPM Joint Research Center for AI), I review how digital twins are shaping **Healthcare 4.0**: how IoT and Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) sensing, artificial intelligence and simulation come together to build them, and where they are being used.
