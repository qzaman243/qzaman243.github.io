Hello! I am Qamar Zaman, a Computer Engineer. I completed my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan.

I am currently working with Dr. Sagheer Khan (SDAIA–KFUPM Joint Research Center for Artificial Intelligence, King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals) on the Internet of Things (IoT), the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) and digital twins for healthcare.

My research focuses on contactless human sensing with WiFi. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Dr. Muhammad Salman and Dr. Sagheer Khan on occupancy detection using WiFi Channel State Information (CSI). I built a real-time sensing system on a Raspberry Pi with Nexmon. This work led to a first-author journal paper, PiSense, in Computing (Springer, 2026), and a public CSI dataset on IEEE DataPort.

I also have hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning and NLP. My hardware background includes embedded Linux and edge AI deployment.

selected publications

  1. Computing
    PiSense: a Raspberry Pi-based real-time occupancy detection system using CSI from commodity WiFi
    Qamar Zaman, Muhammad Ahmad, Sagheer Khan, and Muhammad Salman
    Computing, Oct 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    PiSense is a low-cost, real-time occupancy detection system that uses WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) captured passively on a Raspberry Pi with the Nexmon toolchain. CSI is cleaned with DC/null-subcarrier removal and Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) filtering, and features are extracted either automatically with an autoencoder (semi-supervised) or as maximum eigenvalues (supervised), before a trained ML/DL model decides in real time whether a room is occupied.

  2. IEEE DataPort
    CSI Dataset for WiFi Based Human Detection
    Qamar Zaman, Muhammad Ahmad, Muhammad Salman, and Sagheer Khan
    Jun 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Time-series WiFi Channel State Information (amplitude and phase across 64 subcarriers, 2.4 GHz / 20 MHz) collected passively with a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B and the Nexmon CSI toolchain in three indoor rooms of different sizes. Scenarios cover an empty room, one person (static and moving) and multiple people, labelled Occupied / Not Occupied. Includes raw .pcap captures, pre-processed .csv files, Python capture scripts, DWT denoising and Doppler-based motion features.
